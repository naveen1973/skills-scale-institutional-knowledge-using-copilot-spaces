# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Specialists

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Specialists validate software quality, execute test plans, and ensure acceptance criteria are met before work is marked complete. They collaborate with delivery roles to maintain quality standards across the development lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Create and execute unit, integration, and end-to-end test plans
- Validate acceptance criteria and Definition of Done for backlog items
- Identify, document, and triage defects with clear reproduction steps
- Partner with developers to improve test coverage on critical flows
- Support release readiness through smoke test preparation and execution

### Goals
- Ensure high-quality releases with minimal production defects
- Maintain confidence in critical user journeys through reliable test coverage
- Reduce rework by catching issues early in the delivery cycle

### Typical Communication
- Test plans, bug reports, and QA sign-off updates in tracking tools
- QA status updates during standups, sprint planning, and retrospectives
- Release readiness notes and post-test summaries before deployment

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers:** Collaborate on testability, defect resolution, and regression prevention
- **Product Managers:** Validate acceptance criteria and clarify expected user behavior
- **Project Managers:** Communicate quality risks, test timelines, and release readiness status

---

## Stakeholders/Sponsors

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, approve priorities and investment, and ensure the project delivers measurable value aligned with organizational strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Define business outcomes, constraints, and success metrics
- Approve scope, timeline trade-offs, and budget allocations
- Remove organizational blockers and support cross-team alignment
- Review milestones and provide decisions on major changes
- Advocate for outcomes and value realization with leadership

### Goals
- Ensure delivered work aligns with strategic business objectives
- Maximize return on investment while managing delivery risk
- Maintain organizational alignment on priorities and outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Milestone reviews and executive-facing status updates
- Scope, priority, and investment approval decisions
- Escalation discussions for blockers with business impact

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers:** Provide business context for high-impact technical decisions when needed
- **Product Managers:** Align on priorities, value hypotheses, and success criteria
- **Project Managers:** Review status, resolve escalations, and approve scope or schedule shifts

---

## Technical Leads/Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects define technical direction, review architecture and design decisions, and guide implementation to ensure solutions are scalable, secure, and maintainable.

### Responsibilities
- Define architecture, technical standards, and design patterns
- Review technical designs and complex implementation approaches
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and mitigation strategies
- Mentor developers and support consistent engineering practices
- Balance near-term delivery needs with long-term maintainability

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, scalable, and maintainable systems
- Minimize technical debt and reduce costly future rework
- Improve engineering quality through technical leadership and coaching

### Typical Communication
- Architecture decision records, design reviews, and technical proposals
- Risk assessments and trade-off discussions during planning
- Implementation guidance in code reviews and engineering syncs

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers:** Provide design guidance, unblock complex implementation tasks, and mentor on best practices
- **Product Managers:** Evaluate feasibility and trade-offs to align scope with technical constraints
- **Project Managers:** Surface technical risks, dependencies, and sequencing impacts on the plan

---

## QA/Release Managers

### Role Summary
QA/Release Managers coordinate release planning and execution, manage deployment readiness, and oversee production deployment processes to reduce release risk.

### Responsibilities
- Plan release scope, timing, and readiness checkpoints
- Maintain deployment checklists and release sign-off criteria
- Coordinate staging and production deployment activities
- Lead rollback coordination and release incident communications
- Track release metrics and identify opportunities to improve reliability

### Goals
- Execute predictable, low-risk releases
- Minimize deployment-related incidents and recovery time
- Improve release consistency through repeatable processes

### Typical Communication
- Release plans, release notes, and deployment schedules
- Go/no-go updates and sign-off requests before production deploys
- Post-release verification updates and incident follow-ups

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers:** Confirm release readiness, coordinate deployment support, and triage release issues
- **Product Managers:** Align release content, timing, and customer-impact messaging
- **Project Managers:** Coordinate milestone dependencies and communicate release risk/status

---

## Business Analysts (optional for larger teams)

### Role Summary
Business Analysts clarify requirements, document business rules, and bridge communication between stakeholders and delivery teams, especially in larger or more complex initiatives.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit and document detailed business and functional requirements
- Clarify business rules, process flows, and edge cases
- Identify ambiguities, conflicts, or gaps in specifications
- Support refinement of acceptance criteria and backlog readiness
- Trace requirements to delivered outcomes and test scenarios

### Goals
- Reduce ambiguity in requirements and planning artifacts
- Improve delivery predictability by minimizing misunderstanding-driven rework
- Strengthen alignment between business intent and implementation details

### Typical Communication
- Requirement specifications, process diagrams, and clarification notes
- Backlog refinement discussions and acceptance criteria workshops
- Stakeholder interviews and validation sessions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers:** Provide detailed requirement clarifications and business rule context during implementation
- **Product Managers:** Translate product intent into precise, testable requirements
- **Project Managers:** Improve planning accuracy by clarifying scope boundaries and dependency details
